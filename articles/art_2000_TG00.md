# Hardware - The Gathering at Vikingskipet in Hamar, year 2000

**Easter, year 2000**
What: The Gathering 1900 (TG00)
When: 19th - 23rd of April 2000
Where: Vikingskipet, Hamar, Norway

## Wiki

> 2000 "The Gathering 1900 – Follow the Flow" – inspired by the Millennium Bug / 35 Mbit/s / 10/100 Mbit/s, 100 Mbit/s uplink to core / 4,300 attendees, sold out (an estimated 3,500–4,000 left on the waiting list) / "Freestyler" – Bomfunk MCs
