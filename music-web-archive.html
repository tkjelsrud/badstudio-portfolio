Archive • 2009-2014

Music Web Archive

This page condenses an older personal music site that ran as a rough sketchbook for demos, experiments and work-in-progress notes. The original archive spans 2009 to 2014 and mostly lived as a hand-edited table of MP3 bounces, Flash players and short production comments.

Rather than reproducing the whole thing row by row, this version keeps the useful parts: the timeline, the change in tools and a few representative tracks from different stages. It moves from rough hardware sketches into more structured song drafts and later Reaper-based sessions.

I produced these tracks almost solely on hardware at that point, starting mainly with the MPC1000 around 2009 and then moving further into Elektron gear: first Machinedrum, then later Analog Four and Octatrack. Machinedrum was often the centerpiece, especially for rhythmic elements, with parts being mixed and muted in and out while its MIDI tracks sequenced other gear. I think I had a Lexicon MX200 around a lot of the synth sounds as a spatial multi-effect, and the tracks were usually captured either on a Boss Micro BR or straight into the audio interface.
